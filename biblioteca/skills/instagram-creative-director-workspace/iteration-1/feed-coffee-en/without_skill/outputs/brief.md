# Instagram Feed Post — New Single-Origin Ethiopian (Washed Yirgacheffe)

**Client:** Specialty coffee roastery, Lisbon
**Deliverable:** One Instagram feed post (1080×1350, 4:5 portrait — the most real estate the feed allows)
**Goal:** Announce a new single-origin Ethiopian bean and make it feel *premium and editorial*, not like a generic coffee-shop flatlay.

---

## 1. Strategy & Positioning

**The trap to avoid:** Most coffee-shop posts look identical — overhead latte art on a wooden table, a kraft bag with a stamped logo, warm-and-cozy filter, caption full of emojis and "perfect morning ☕" energy. That reads as *café*, not *roaster*. Specialty buyers want provenance, craft, and restraint.

**The angle we'll own:** Treat the bean like a *vintage* — origin, process, and tasting notes presented like a sommelier card. The post should feel closer to a perfume launch or a natural-wine label than a café menu. Premium = white space, confidence, fewer words, one strong image, typography doing the heavy lifting.

**One-line creative idea:**
> *"A washed Yirgacheffe that tastes like jasmine and lemon peel — presented with the calm of a gallery label."*

**Tone:** Quiet confidence. Sensory, specific, sparse. Portuguese-Lisbon roastery sophistication — European, not American-cozy.

---

## 2. The Visual (art direction for the photographer / designer)

**Format:** 4:5 portrait, 1080×1350.

**Composition — pick ONE, do not crowd:**

- **Option A (recommended) — The Spill.**
  A small, precise scatter of *raw green + roasted* beans on a seamless, cool-toned backdrop (bone, stone-grey, or deep espresso-black — NOT warm wood). Shot from a slight 3/4 angle, hard directional light from one side casting long, sculptural shadows. Beans occupy the lower third; the upper two-thirds is breathing room for type. Macro sharpness on a few beans, the rest falling soft.

- **Option B — The Pour, abstracted.**
  Extreme close-up of a single drip/pour-over, backlit so the coffee glows amber-garnet. Almost abstract. Floral element (a single jasmine sprig or lemon twist) resting at the edge — a literal nod to the tasting notes, used sparingly.

- **Option C — The Label.**
  The new bag photographed like a product hero on a plinth of stone, single softbox, deep shadow, minimal. Lets the packaging design carry the premium feel.

**Art-direction rules (the things that make it NOT generic):**
- No warm "cozy café" filter. Go cool and editorial, or rich and moody. Pick a clear direction.
- One light source, intentional shadow. Flat even lighting = amateur.
- Negative space is the luxury signal. Resist filling the frame.
- A single botanical prop (jasmine OR lemon peel) max — it earns its place by mirroring the tasting notes. Don't stage a whole still-life.
- No latte art. No hands holding a mug. No laptop-and-coffee desk scene.

**Color palette:**
- Base: bone white `#F4F1EC` or near-black espresso `#1C1816`
- Accent 1 (floral): muted jasmine cream / pale ivory
- Accent 2 (citrus): a restrained warm yellow `#D9A441` used in TINY doses (a line, a dot, the notes)
- Avoid: saturated browns, orange "coffee" clichés, multiple competing colors.

---

## 3. On-Image Typography (the layout)

Treat it like a tasting card / gallery label. Suggested stack, top to bottom:

```
SINGLE ORIGIN · ETHIOPIA            ← small caps, letter-spaced, quiet (eyebrow)

Yirgacheffe                          ← large serif display, the hero word

washed · floral · citrus             ← thin caps, the notes

—

[ now roasting ]                     ← tiny tag, lower corner
```

**Type direction:**
- Display: an elegant serif with character (e.g. Canela, GT Sectra, Reckless, or a clean Didone) — this single choice does most of the "premium" work.
- Supporting: a fine, wide-tracked sans (e.g. a light grotesque) in ALL CAPS for the eyebrow and notes.
- Strict hierarchy: one hero word, everything else whispers. Generous tracking on the small text.
- Left-aligned or centered — commit to one. No drop shadows, no gradients on text.

---

## 4. The Caption

Keep it short, confident, sensory. No emoji clutter (one, max, or none). Lead with the experience, not the announcement.

**Primary caption:**

> Jasmine on the nose. Lemon peel on the finish.
>
> Our new single-origin lands this week: a washed **Yirgacheffe** from the highlands of southern Ethiopia — bright, floral, and clean enough to drink black and slow.
>
> Picked ripe, washed, sun-dried. Roasted light, here in Lisbon, to keep every note intact.
>
> Limited first roast. In the shop and online now.
>
> —
> #Yirgacheffe #SingleOrigin #SpecialtyCoffee #EthiopianCoffee #LisbonCoffee #CafeLisboa #WashedProcess #LightRoast #ThirdWaveCoffee #CoffeeRoastery

**Alt caption (shorter / more editorial):**

> Yirgacheffe.
> Washed. Floral. Citrus.
> A single-origin from southern Ethiopia, roasted light in Lisbon this week.
> Drink it black. Take your time.

**Hashtag note:** mix 3–4 high-intent niche tags (#Yirgacheffe, #WashedProcess) with 3–4 local (#LisbonCoffee, #CafeLisboa) and a couple broad-reach (#SpecialtyCoffee). Put them on a separate line or first comment to keep the caption clean.

---

## 5. Optional Carousel Extension (recommended)

A single post works, but for a launch a 3-slide carousel deepens the premium "story" feel:

1. **Slide 1 — Hero:** the visual + "Yirgacheffe" type card (above). The scroll-stopper.
2. **Slide 2 — Provenance:** a clean map dot / single line of text — *"Yirgacheffe, Gedeo Zone · ~1,900m · washed process."* White space heavy.
3. **Slide 3 — Tasting:** the notes as a sommelier card — *"Jasmine · Lemon peel · Black tea · Silky body"* + a one-line brew tip (*"Best as pour-over or black filter. 1:16, just off the boil."*) and the CTA.

---

## 6. Production Checklist

- [ ] Shoot/select ONE strong image, cool or moody — no warm café filter.
- [ ] One intentional light source + real shadow.
- [ ] Negative space preserved (type lives in the breathing room).
- [ ] Max one botanical prop (jasmine or lemon), mirroring the notes.
- [ ] Serif hero word + tracked sans supporting type, strict hierarchy.
- [ ] Caption: sensory-first, ≤4 short lines, emoji restraint.
- [ ] CTA present (in shop + online, "limited first roast").
- [ ] Hashtags: niche + local + reach, kept out of the main caption body.
- [ ] First comment: brew recommendation (pour-over, 1:16) for the specialty crowd.

**Best posting window (Lisbon, specialty audience):** weekday morning, ~08:00–10:00, or Saturday morning before the shop fills.

---

## 7. Why this is premium, not generic (rationale)

- **Provenance over product:** we sell the *story of the bean* (altitude, process, region), which is what specialty buyers actually pay for.
- **Restraint as a luxury cue:** white space, one image, one hero word — the opposite of the cluttered café feed.
- **Sensory specificity:** "jasmine, lemon peel, black tea" beats "delicious aromatic coffee." Specific = credible = premium.
- **Art direction discipline:** cool/moody light + editorial typography is the single biggest visual differentiator from the warm-flatlay default everyone else posts.
